About the encounter

One family, gathered from every corner of the world

Embark on a transformative journey at the Augustinian Youth Encounter 2027 (AYE27) in South Korea. This global gathering brings together the young hearts of the Augustinian Family from every corner of the world to celebrate our shared faith and community.

Taking place from August 8–13, 2027, AYE27 is a sacred invitation to deepen your relationship with Christ and rediscover the power of the Augustinian charism — a time to renew our identity as a global community, proving that though we come from different nations, we move as one.

Walking Together in Seoul

Set against the vibrant backdrop of South Korea, AYE27 begins immediately following World Youth Day 2027 in Seoul — a once-in-a-lifetime chance to walk together, one mind and one heart, on our collective way toward God. Our theme echoes WYD Seoul's own call to "take courage" (John 16:33).
