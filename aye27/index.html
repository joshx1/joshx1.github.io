About the encounter

One family, gathered from every corner of the world

Augustinian Youth Encounters began in 1987 to accompany young adults growing in life and faith through Augustinian communities worldwide. AYE27 hopes to gather 300–400 young people in South Korea — the first Encounter since the election of Pope Leo XIV, an Augustinian and son of Saint Augustine, and a moment to rediscover the charism's beauty in the service of the Church.
